Privacy

Pre-production draft — information to be verified before publication.

Controller

To complete: VENDEUR_LEGAL, To complete: ADRESSE, privacy contact: Maxlolgames@proton.me.

Data and purposes

Data inventory: (1) site visit: hosting provider technical logs (IP address, requested pages); (2) purchase through the payment service: order ID, product, status and dates, PayPal transaction ID, licence key; the payer's e-mail is kept only as a non-reversible fingerprint, for purchase restoration; (3) abuse protection: IP address kept only as a fingerprint with an hourly counter; (4) player account (prototype, not enabled): display name, saves, save history, device ID; (5) local saves: they stay on your device and are not transmitted. Processing, purposes, legal basis, whether mandatory and consequences of not providing the data: providing the site and security (technical data; proposed legal basis: legitimate interest); fulfilling purchases and restoration (order and licence data; proposed legal basis: performance of the contract); abuse prevention (IP fingerprint; legitimate interest); player account, once enabled (display name, saves; performance of the requested service). These legal bases are to be validated To complete: VALIDATION_BASES_LEGALES. Order data is required to obtain the purchase; without it, it cannot be delivered. Local saves are distinct from data sent to a server.

Recipients and payments

Providers actually used and their roles: GitHub (site hosting), Netlify (hosting of the games and payment service), PayPal (payment), itch.io (third-party store, when you buy through itch.io, under its own terms) and the player-account database (prototype, not enabled). PayPal has its own privacy policy for its processing.
