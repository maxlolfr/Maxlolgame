Gravity Turn

Place. Turn. Cascade!

Place jelly blocks on an 8×8 grid: every three pieces, the board turns a quarter and everything falls. A line that makes another one fall is a cascade.

FAQ

Is Gravity Turn free?

Yes, and the game offers no purchases for now.

Which devices?

In the browser, on phone and computer. An iPhone version is announced.

Which languages?

French and English.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically. The save is kept in the browser of the device you use: it is not shared between two devices and is lost if you clear the browser data. You can move your progress with the game's save code.

How does the rotation work?

Every three pieces you place, the board turns a quarter and the blocks fall.
