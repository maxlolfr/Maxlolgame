Five reasons to play

🔥

A torch that fades

Four levels, from Clarity to Total Darkness: the darker it gets, the harder monsters hit. In total darkness, loot is tripled.

⚔️

Turn-based combat

Two rows, Guard, elemental weaknesses and full-screen ultimates: every turn counts.

🛡️

8 heroes, 28 costumes

Build a team of three and unlock 28 Links between heroes. The 8 heroes have 28 costumes and voices during battles.

👹

20 bosses that change phase

At half health they enrage, act twice or summon reinforcements.

♾️

Always one more descent

Ascensions, Infinite Depths, a daily challenge and a weekly challenge.

FAQ

Is the demo really free?

Yes. The demo is free, needs no account and plays right in your phone or computer browser.

What does the full game add to the demo?

The demo covers worlds 1 to 5. The full game opens all 20 worlds and their 20 bosses.

How much is the full game?

€7.99, as a one-time purchase, with no subscription.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically, on the device you use. If you clear your browser data, the save is gone.

Which devices and languages?

Phone and computer, in the browser. The game is in French, English, Spanish and Portuguese.
