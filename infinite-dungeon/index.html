FAQ

Is Infinite Dungeon free?

Yes, and the game offers no purchases for now.

Is the game finished?

No, it is version 0.8, still in development.

Do I play the fights?

No, they play out on their own. You choose the relics, the gear and the doors.

Which devices and languages?

In the browser, on phone and computer. The game is in French and English.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically. The save is kept in the browser of the device you use: it is not shared between two devices and is lost if you clear the browser data.
