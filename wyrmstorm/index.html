FAQ

Is WYRMSTORM really free?

Yes. You can play for free, with no account, right in your browser.

Is the shop required?

No. It offers hero costumes and optional offers.

Are there real-money purchases?

WYRMSTORM is a free game with optional in-app purchases. Payments are not operational yet: they will be available soon. Meanwhile you can play without paying anything.

Which devices?

Phone (it plays with one thumb) and computer, in the browser.

Which languages?

French, English and Thai.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically. The save is kept in the browser of the device you use: it is not shared between two devices and is lost if you clear the browser data. You can move your progress with the game's save code.
