Chibi Legends

Your heroes fight. You build the team.

Chibi Legends is an idle action RPG: you build the team, your heroes do the fighting. Recruit 8 heroes, pick 4, pair them in duos and climb 400 levels across 20 worlds, each guarded by a boss that changes tactics mid-fight. Upgrade, equip, learn skills, then be reborn stronger. Even when you are away, your team keeps earning gold.

Reasons to play

⚔️

Heroes that fight for you

8 heroes, a team of 4 (70 line-ups), 3 skills and an ultimate each. You pick who fights and when the ultimates fire.

🤝

28 duo links

Put the right two heroes side by side and their ultimates charge a link gauge: when it is full, a combined attack with its own cutscene.

🌍

20 worlds, 400 levels

From the Forgotten Crypt to the Heart of the Night. A boss every 5 levels, and the climb goes on past level 400.

👹

20 bosses that change tactics

Each boss announces a big attack, then switches to a second phase at half health: bone wall, rage, summons, regeneration, a second life…

📖

130 monsters in the bestiary

104 monsters, 20 bosses and 6 elites. Six elements, weaknesses, resistances and 10 behaviours (poison, vampirism, dodge, theft…). Complete entries for +1% gold each.

🎒

Loot, relics and rebirth

6 gear slots, 5 rarities up to Mythic, 22 permanent relics, Ember talents and rebirth to start over stronger.

⏳

It earns while you are away

Your team keeps earning gold when you are not playing, up to 8 hours. Daily quests, achievements and a daily merchant bring you back.

🎧

Music and voices

A jukebox of 16 tracks unlocked world by world, voices for the heroes, a pause menu and an audio mixer.

FAQ

Can I try Chibi Legends now?

Yes. The demo is playable right now in your browser, with no account.

Do I have to play every fight?

No. Your heroes fight on their own. You upgrade them, equip gear and unleash the ultimates when you want to.

Does my team earn anything while I am away?

Yes. Your team keeps earning gold when you are away, up to 8 hours.

How long is the game?

There are 20 worlds and 400 levels, with a boss every 5 levels, and the climb goes on beyond. Rebirth lets you restart stronger, so there is always a new run to try.

Why replay it?

Eight heroes make 70 possible teams, 28 duo links change how fights play out, loot goes up to Mythic, and there are 22 relics, Ember talents and 130 bestiary entries to complete.

Which devices?

Phone and computer, in the browser.

Which languages?

French, English, Spanish, Portuguese and Thai.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. There is no account and no server: clearing your browser data erases the save.
